## Register a New Account

<a href="https://cloud.phala.com/register" class="button primary">Register Now</a>

Sign up with **GitHub**, **Google**, or **email and password**. Registration takes under a minute.

After registration, set up your workspace. Add a card, or skip it and top up your Balance instead. When you add a card, we charge $1 to check it and refund it right after. Some new workspaces are offered a welcome credit when they add a card, while the offer lasts.

Your Balance pays for CVMs, GPU instances, and Private AI. See [How do I pay?](/phala-cloud/faqs#how-do-i-pay-for-cvms-gpu-instances-and-private-ai) for details.
