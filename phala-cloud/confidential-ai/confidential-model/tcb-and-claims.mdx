---
title: TCB and Claims
description: Interpret typed claims in receipts and attested sessions, including source, status, and TCB freshness.
---

Confidential responses carry typed claims about the upstream that served the request, including TCB (Trusted Computing Base) freshness. Claims appear in the attested session referenced by the receipt's `upstream.verified.session_id`; the canonical receipt event does not embed the full claim set.

A claim is not just a boolean. It has a status, a source, and often a reason.

```json
{
  "tee_attested": {
    "status": "asserted",
    "source": "hardware_proven",
    "reason": "verified TEE quote and bound request channel"
  }
}
```

## Claim Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `asserted` | Evidence backs this claim. |
| `refuted` | Evidence contradicts this claim, such as a stale TCB. |
| `unknown` | The verifier did not establish this claim. Treat it as not proven. |

## Claim Source

| Source | Assurance |
| --- | --- |
| `hardware_proven` | Comes from verified TEE quote or collateral. |
| `verifier_derived` | Computed by the verifier from checked evidence. |
| `provider_asserted` | Published by the provider, not independently proven. |
| `operator_asserted` | Declared by the gateway operator. |

## Common Claims

| Claim | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `tee_attested` | An attested TEE identity is bound to the channel, directly or through the measured proxy; inspect `source`. |
| `tcb_up_to_date` | The platform Trusted Computing Base is current. |
| `os_known_good` | The OS or platform image maps to reviewed source or known measurements. |
| `serving_software_known_good` | Serving software meets the verifier’s acceptance policy, such as Sigstore provenance or an operator measurement pin. Inspect the reason. |
| `gpu_attested` | Provider-specific GPU check outcome; inspect the verifier and reason before treating it as authenticated evidence. |
| `model_weights_provenance` | Model weights and quantization match a reviewed provenance chain. |

Privatemode reports `tee_attested: asserted` with `source: verifier_derived` under verifier
`privatemode-proxy/co-deployed-contrast/v1`. It relies on the measured co-deployed proxy and its
Contrast-attested secret path, rather than a provider quote verified directly by the gateway.
Its other common claims are `unknown`.

## TCB Freshness

For verifiers that expose a TCB status, `tcb_up_to_date` is tri-state:

- Up-to-date evidence asserts the claim.
- Stale evidence refutes the claim and records the stale status.
- Missing evidence leaves the claim `unknown`.

Tinfoil is an exception: its verifier requires an up-to-date TCB but exposes no separate TCB status, so the claim is asserted as `verifier_derived`. Your policy decides whether a refuted TCB is acceptable; inspect the cited session’s reason and source.

## GPU Attestation

The assurance depends on the provider verifier. For Phala and Chutes, `gpu_attested: asserted` records an accepted, nonce-matched supplemental NVIDIA check. Those paths decode JWTs without verifying their signatures, so this claim does not authenticate GPU identity or bind it to the serving CPU TEE.

ArmetAI instead requires an NRAS-signed GPU result and commits the exact GPU evidence into the CPU quote’s report data. Its claim has the same `verifier_derived` source label, but a different evidence boundary. Inspect the reason and raw session evidence. Neither path proves model-weight provenance or continued use of a particular GPU for your request. See [Providers](/phala-cloud/confidential-ai/confidential-model/providers).

## Suggested Policy

1. For direct hardware proof, require `tee_attested: asserted` from `hardware_proven`. This policy excludes Privatemode's `verifier_derived` sessions.
2. Require `upstream.verified.result = verified` and `required = true` for sensitive prompts.
3. Treat `unknown` as not proven.
4. Decide whether to reject `tcb_up_to_date: refuted`.
5. Appraise the reason and provider-specific evidence for `serving_software_known_good` and `gpu_attested`; an asserted status alone does not establish provenance or authenticated GPU identity.
